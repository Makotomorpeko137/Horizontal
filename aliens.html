DECONSTRUCTED

THEY WALK AMONG US

For years, neofascists and nationalists have spread the myth
that refugees and migrants are the real threat.

For years we were told that America had made progress. The Emancipation Proclamation. The abolition of slavery. The Civil Rights Movement. Years of struggle for equality and dignity.

Yet under Donald Trump, many of these hard-won gains feel under attack again.

  • He called Mexican immigrants “rapists” and “criminals” when he launched his campaign.
  • His administration separated thousands of migrant children from their parents at the border.
  • He repeatedly attacked Black Lives Matter and called protesters “thugs”.
  • He has praised some of the most extreme far-right figures and movements.
  • His rhetoric has encouraged and energized white nationalist groups.
  • He continues to push mass deportations and policies that treat migrants as invaders rather than human beings.
  • He incited the January 6 Capitol attack and spent months pushing the “Big Lie” that the 2020 election was stolen.
  • He was impeached twice and has been found liable for sexual abuse in civil court.
  • His tax cuts overwhelmingly benefited the rich and corporations while ballooning the national debt.
  • He denied climate change and pulled the U.S. out of the Paris Agreement.
  • His administration mishandled the COVID-19 pandemic, contributing to hundreds of thousands of preventable deaths.

Open your eyes.
Instead of “Make America Great Again”, we are getting policies that deepen division, fear, and inequality.

The Trump administration has officially designated Antifa as a terrorist organization. President Trump described its actions as a “sick and dangerous” element of the radical left.

At the same time, reports continue of excessive force by ICE, sometimes linked to discrimination based on sexual orientation or gender identity.

If the Right can kill LGBTQ+ people, does that mean the Left can open fire on heterosexual people?
No. Killing people is fundamentally wrong — regardless of which side does it. Violence is not the answer.

The promise of progress is fragile. When fear and division become the main political tools, rights can be rolled back — especially for those at the bottom.

This is not about one man. It’s about a system that allows authoritarian tendencies and xenophobia to rise again and again.
